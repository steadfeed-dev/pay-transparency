Laws we track

State and city pay-transparency rules, with official sources.

Including Washington, California, Colorado, New York, Illinois, Minnesota, Maryland, Massachusetts, New Jersey, Vermont, and the 2026 additions Connecticut, Virginia and Maine, plus local rules such as New York City and Jersey City. Read the state law pages.
