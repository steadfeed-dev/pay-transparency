How we build

Plain tools, honest output.

Pay per result

No plans, seats or subscriptions. Each tool lists its price per result on its Apify Store page, and you can set a maximum cost per run.

Your account, your data

Runs happen in your own Apify account. Our tools work from public pages, public feeds and any API keys you choose to add. They do not log in to your systems or get around paywalls.

Careful wording

Findings are labeled as signals to review, not verdicts. Nothing here is legal advice or a compliance certificate.
