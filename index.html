Who it is for

Built for the people who have to watch the postings.

If you publish or review job ads across states, provinces or countries, this gives you a repeating screen of the public boards you already use — without logging into an ATS.

HR and talent-acquisition teams

A weekly check that a pay range has not dropped off a live posting, or that a new role went out without one.

Recruiting agencies and HR consultants

One watchlist per client, on the client’s public Greenhouse, Lever or Ashby board. No access to their systems.

People-operations and compliance reviewers

A digest and report that names the laws screened and links each source, so a person can review the live posting.

How it works

Read the public board. Screen the posting. Watch it over time.

01

Point it at a careers board

Paste public Greenhouse, Lever or Ashby board URLs. The monitor reads the same public job feeds the careers site uses, then checks the posting page if the feed shows no range.

02

Screen for potential issues

Each posting is compared with in-force US, Canadian and EU posting rules that match the location text. Statuses stay cautious: potential issue, review suggested, no issue found, no law identified, or unchecked.

03

Re-check on a schedule

Run the same watchlist weekly (daily is supported). You are told when a range disappears, a new posting has none, or a flagged item is resolved. Optional webhooks can send the report onward.

Coverage

2026 posting rules, kept as a screen — not a certificate.

Laws are matched from the posting’s location text. Remote US roles can be screened against the states you hire in, or every in-force state law. Employer-size thresholds are skipped when you provide a headcount. Upcoming laws stay silent until their effective date.

US states: CA, CO, CT, DC, HI, IL, ME, MD, MA, MN, NJ, NY, VT, VA, WA US local: New York City, Westchester County, Cleveland, Jersey City (5+ employees) Upcoming: Columbus, Ohio (posting duty enforceable 1 Jan 2027); Delaware (26 Sep 2027) Canada: British Columbia, Ontario, Prince Edward Island, New Brunswick EU: Pay Transparency Directive note, applied through each member state’s law

Honest limits

This is a screening aid for public posting text. It cannot see ranges shown only as images, PDFs, behind a login, or on pages that need JavaScript. It does not read Indeed, LinkedIn or other copies of a posting. Workday, iCIMS and custom career pages are not supported yet. This is not an official ATS partnership or legal advice.

How to read results

Every flag is a prompt to look, not a finding of violation.

Potential issue

A high-priority item was found in the data we can read, for example no pay range. Open the live posting and confirm before acting.

Review suggested

Wording is unclear, or a lower-priority item such as benefits or a close date may be missing where a law asks for it.

No issue found

Nothing was flagged by this screen. This is not a statement that the posting complies.

No law identified / Unchecked

No matching law could be read from the location text, or the lookup failed. Unchecked items are never billed and never counted as issues.
