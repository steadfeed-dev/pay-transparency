Limitations

An honest screen, with honest limits.

This is a screening aid for public posting text. Every flag is a prompt to look, so a person should review the live posting before acting.

  • It cannot see pay ranges shown only as images or PDFs.
  • It cannot see anything behind a login, or on pages that need JavaScript to show the range.
  • It does not read Indeed, LinkedIn or other copies of a posting. Workday, iCIMS and custom career pages are not supported yet.
  • Facts such as employer size, where the worker will be based and exemptions can decide whether a law applies. A screen can’t see them.

Not legal advice

Findings are potential issues for human review. “No issue found” means this screen flagged nothing, not that a posting meets any law. Laws and thresholds change, so where it matters, check with counsel. There is no ATS partnership here: Steadfeed reads public job feeds only.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Is this legal advice?

No. It is a screening aid for public posting text. Findings are potential issues for human review, and where it matters you should check with counsel.

Do I need to connect or log into my ATS?

No. The monitor reads public job feeds for Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby boards, the same ones a careers site uses, and then checks the posting page if the feed shows no range. It needs no access to your systems and is not an ATS partnership.

How often does it check?

Weekly is the default and daily is available. You can schedule the same watchlist in your Apify account, and set a maximum cost per run.

What does it cost?

$0.02 for an initial or changed posting check and $0.005 for a verified unchanged recheck, plus a $0.00005 actor start and normal Apify platform usage. It is pay per use, with no subscription.

What does “No issue found” mean?

Only that nothing was flagged by this screen. It is not a statement that a posting complies with any law.

Which laws and job boards are covered?

US state and local laws including NJ, Jersey City (5+ employees), VT, MA, Cleveland, CT and ME, with Columbus, Ohio from 1 January 2027. Canada: British Columbia, Ontario, Prince Edward Island and New Brunswick. The EU directive is included as a note. See the coverage section for the full list. Boards: public Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby.

What can’t it see?

Pay ranges shown only as images or PDFs, behind a login, or on pages that need JavaScript. It does not read Indeed, LinkedIn or other copies of a posting.

What does this website collect?

Nothing personal. It sets no cookies and loads no analytics or third-party scripts. See the privacy note.
