EU Pay Transparency Directive: Article 9 indicators

The Directive measures women's and men's pay. Non-binary employees are counted separately and left out of these ratios. Hourly pay = (base + variable) ÷ annual hours. A category gets the 5% flag when the mean gap is 5% or more either way. Under Art. 10, a gap like that that you can't justify on objective, gender-neutral grounds, and haven't fixed within six months, triggers a joint pay assessment. First reports are due June 7, 2027 (yearly for 250+ workers; every three years for 150–249). This is an analysis aid and does not file with any EU authority.

Questions

What is the California SB 464 pay data report 2027?

Starting with Reporting Year 2026, due May 12, 2027, California SB 464 requires covered employers to classify employees into 23 SOC-based job categories instead of the 10 EEO-1 categories. Official CRD filing templates are scheduled for February 2027. CRD published preliminary planning templates on November 16, 2026; they should not be filed.

What are the 23 SOC job categories?

CRD’s November 16, 2026 preliminary Job Category Mapping Table uses 11-1010 for chief executives, 11-M000 for other management occupations, then 13-0000 through 53-0000 for the remaining groups. Military (SOC 55) is not a reporting category.
