Privacy

This site does not collect personal data.

This is a static website. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and loads no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers. Fonts are hosted with the site. Links to the Apify Store include ordinary campaign parameters (source, medium, campaign, content) so we can see, in Apify’s own traffic reports, which button was used. Those parameters are not used to identify you on this site.

The product itself runs on the Apify platform, in your own Apify account, and screens public job postings you ask it to check. How Apify handles account and run data is described in Apify’s privacy policy.

We do not use this site to collect names, emails, payment details or résumés. For product questions, use the Issues tab on the Apify Store page, or write to hello@getsteadfeed.com.
