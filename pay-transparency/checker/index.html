FAQ

Questions, answered

Is my job posting uploaded anywhere?

No. The check runs as JavaScript inside your browser tab. The text you paste is not sent to Steadfeed or anyone else, and it is gone when you close or reload the page. Our only analytics is cookie-free Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page visits and never sees what you type.

Which laws does it check?

Every US state and city law in the table above that is in force today: 22 entries, including Colorado, California, Washington, New York State and City, Illinois, Maryland, Minnesota, Vermont, Massachusetts, New Jersey, Hawaii, Washington D.C., Connecticut, Maine, Virginia, Jersey City, Ithaca, Westchester County and Cleveland. Columbus, Ohio (Jan 1, 2027) and Delaware (Sep 26, 2027) switch on automatically on their start dates.

Does “DOE” or “competitive salary” count?

No. These laws ask for real numbers, usually a good-faith minimum and maximum. Phrases like “DOE”, “competitive” or “negotiable” are fine to add, but only next to a range.

What about remote jobs?

A remote job that someone in a covered state could take can be reached by that state’s law. Several states and New York City say so in their guidance. Mark the job as remote and the checker screens it against every state law in force, then shows what each one needs.

Is this legal advice?

No. It is a quick screen of the posting text, built on public statutes and agency guidance. Employer-size counts, exceptions and remote-work rules vary. Check the official source linked for each law, or ask an employment lawyer before relying on the result.
